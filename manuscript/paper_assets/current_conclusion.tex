The current shared GRACE-RUL pipeline combines related-channel fault simulation, independent detection, bounded recurrent recovery and local/global RUL prediction. Validation selects weights and conservative thresholds before calibration and testing. Official capped RMSE is 12.01 cycles and severity-0.2 stuck RMSE is 15.08; the full recovery matrix bounds observed intact displacement on held selection contexts. Observable scaling preserves one simultaneous calibration score per engine. Explicit failure analysis, separate coverage units and immutable provenance delimit the evidence. A post-freeze ablation attributes the persistent-fault gain to the recovery path and finds no measurable RUL benefit from the bounded corrector. These results support an auditable benchmark study; they do not establish universal sensor recovery, zero severe prediction errors, superiority over matched re-implementations of the compared methods, or real-fleet safety.
