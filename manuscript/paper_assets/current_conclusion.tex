The format-10 GRACE-RUL pipeline combines related-channel fault simulation, independent detection, probability-weighted support-bounded recovery and local/global RUL prediction with an observed engine-age channel. Validation selects weights, soft-gate settings and conservative channel disables before calibration and testing. Official capped RMSE is 11.08 cycles and severity-0.2 stuck RMSE is 12.11; the complete recovery matrix bounds observed intact displacement on held selection contexts. Observable scaling preserves one simultaneous calibration score per engine. Explicit failure analysis, separate coverage units and immutable provenance delimit the evidence. The bounded corrector is disabled after a null component ablation, detector recall remains low on several finite families, and the capped target leaves a large raw long-horizon bias. The monotone plateau stretch was available, but its non-increasing capped-RMSE guard rejected every positive value (alpha=0), so the raw-target bias is not reduced. These results support an auditable benchmark study; they do not establish universal sensor recovery, zero severe prediction errors, superiority over matched re-implementations of the compared methods, or real-fleet safety.
